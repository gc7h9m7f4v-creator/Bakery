A bakery made for the neighborhood

Our story

North Star Bakery brings people together with fresh bread, welcoming service, and a menu shaped by the seasons.

Handmade, every morning

Our bakers mix, shape, and bake in small batches so the counter feels fresh when neighbors arrive. From a loaf for the week to a cake for a family gathering, we make the everyday feel a little more special.

We keep our menu focused and make room for seasonal bakes, with pre-orders available for customers planning ahead.

Thoughtful ingredients

Our sourcing philosophy

We prioritize quality ingredients and look to nearby farms and local suppliers when seasonal ingredients are available. Our team plans each bake around freshness, flavor, and the care that goes into making it by hand.
